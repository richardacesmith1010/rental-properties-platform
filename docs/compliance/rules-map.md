## What Domus must not do in PA
1. Let a deposit exceed two months' rent in year 1 or one month after, or honor a tenant waiver.
2. Let day 30 pass without an itemized damage list and refund (double damages).
3. Call an app message, email or text a "notice to quit," or say it starts the 10-day clock.
4. Treat online payment as consent to e-notices or e-signing, or block download and print of signed leases.
5. From Dec. 21, 2026: allow an application fee above actual cost, add a Domus margin, or change parking fees mid-lease.
6. List vacancies, refer applicants, negotiate leases or manage homes; onboard paid PA managers without license attestation.
7. Turn on automatic or daily-stacking late fees in PA.
8. Prompt rent or lease changes on a Lower Merion unit with an open violation or after a tenant complaint.
