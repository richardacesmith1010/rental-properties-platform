Questions

Is Domus really free?

Yes, while we are in early access. You don't need a credit card to start.

How do tenants pay?

Online, by bank or card, through Stripe. They can turn on autopay.

Is my data safe?

Each person only sees what they should. Connections are encrypted. Changes are logged.

What if a tenant pays late?

Domus sends reminders. You see what is overdue and for how long.

Can my property manager use it?

Yes. Managers get their own view of just the homes they run.
